# UI and UX: before and after

Screenshots are from the same demo data (the sample course pack, see `scripts/ui-demo.ts`). Before: [`docs/ui-ux/before/`](ui-ux/before/). After: [`docs/ui-ux/after/`](ui-ux/after/).

| Screen | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Projects ([before](ui-ux/before/before-01-projects.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-01-projects.png)) | A table: name, raw URL, scan count, last status. Nothing to do for a new user. | Cards with description, readable address plus "Copy full address", latest scan with status and date, and one next action: "Start first scan" or "View latest results". Search appears once there are more than four projects. |
| Project ([before](ui-ux/before/before-02-project.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-02-project.png)) | Upload, a 20-field scan form, Excel links and history on one page. | Tabs: Overview, Course files, Scan history. "Start new scan" and "Download tracker" in the header. |
| New scan ([after](ui-ux/after/after-08-new-scan.png), [checks](ui-ux/after/after-12-new-scan-checks.png), [review](ui-ux/after/after-13-new-scan-review.png)) | Origins, path prefixes, timeouts, states, depth, baseline and sizes in one form. | Three stages: Choose your course (link or ZIP), Choose checks (plain-language list, screen sizes, optional client settings), Review and start. Technical controls sit under Advanced settings with the brief's plain labels. |
| Results ([before](ui-ux/before/before-03-results.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-03-results.png)) | Title was the URL. Everything on one long page. | Title is the captured course name. "Scan finished — review the results" with the coverage limit beside it. Tabs: Summary, Issues, Coverage, Manual review, Technical details. Three counts with their unit, and a separate "checks that did not run" line. |
| Issues ([after](ui-ux/after/after-09-issues.png)) | Grouped lists with tiny thumbnails. | Filterable, searchable, paginated cards: where, what to do, status and owner with Saved feedback, retest beside them. |
| Evidence ([after](ui-ux/after/after-14-evidence-viewer.png)) | Thumbnails of whole-page screenshots, hard to read. | Click to open a viewer: fit, actual size, zoom, original in a new tab, Escape to close. Missing files are explained. |
| Coverage ([before](ui-ux/before/before-04-coverage.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-04-coverage.png)) | Lower down the page, away from the completed banner. | A plain statement of what was and was not checked at the top of the tab and a short version next to the status. No coverage percentage. |
| Finding ([before](ui-ux/before/before-05-finding.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-05-finding.png)) | Observed, expected, remediation, with fingerprints. | What happened, why it matters, what to do, where, owner and status, evidence viewer; rule details in a disclosure. |
| Technical ([after](ui-ux/after/after-10-technical.png)) | All check executions in one long table. | Counts with units, searchable and filterable executions, 50 per page. |
| Downloads ([after](ui-ux/after/after-15-download-menu.png)) | Four loose links. | "Download report" menu: Excel issue tracker, PDF, HTML, JSON under technical exports. Each says what it covers; preparing and failure messages are shown. |
| Help ([after](ui-ux/after/after-11-help.png)) | "About this installation" capability table. | Help: how to check a course, what results do and do not mean, and System status that says Ready or Not ready first. |
| Small screens ([before](ui-ux/before/before-07-results-mobile.png), [after](ui-ux/after/after-07-results-mobile.png)); [200% zoom](ui-ux/after/after-16-results-200-percent-zoom.png) | Worked, but long. | Navigation and tabs wrap; wide tables scroll inside their own region; no sideways page scroll (tested at 390 px). |
